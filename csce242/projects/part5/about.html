About

Built for the people who run the gym floor.

Cano Tech — short for Canopus Technologies — started with a simple problem: membership businesses were managing their most important data across spreadsheets, sticky notes, and disconnected booking systems.

We built Cano Tech to give gyms, studios, and wellness operators a single, clear place to store and manage every member record. No algorithms, no predictions — just your data, organized and accessible.
